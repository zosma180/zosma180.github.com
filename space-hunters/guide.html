Complete walkthrough

This guide explains step by step how to complete the adventure. It contains spoilers. Locations are x-y coordinates relative to the origin of each map, as reported by the Cortex in-game (the first tile is 1-1).

General tips

This game requires careful ammunition management. Avoid wasting shots so you do not run into trouble on later maps. Save some resources for specific areas:

  • Perforator: use it in the external factory.
  • Detonator: use it to take down Tanks; save it for the final maps, such as the power station or laboratory, if possible.
  • Overloader: use it as a fallback to slow down Tanks.
  • Radiator: save it for the final maps, such as the power station and laboratory.

Calypso

  1. Start in the Med Room and complete the movement tutorial by following the instructions.
  2. Pick up the Cortex at 1-2.
  3. Use the door at 2-1 to reach the Connecting Hub.
  4. In the Connecting Hub, enter the Tek Room through the door at 1-2.
  5. In the Tek Room, interact with the shield relic at 5-1.
  6. Interact with the pistol relic at 1-4.
  7. Follow the combat tutorial: take out the Scout with the Plasma first.
  8. Take out the Tank with a Detonator charge (aim, fire the charge, then press Q to detonate it).
  9. Optional: listen to the Equipment recording at 4-6 (stand on the tile and face down).
  10. Use the door at 8-3 to return to the Connecting Hub.
  11. From the Connecting Hub, enter the Calypso Station Control Room through the door at 6-1. The introductory dialogue plays automatically.
  12. Optional: before leaving, listen to the Storm recording at 2-1 (stand on the tile and face up).
  13. Return to the Hub through the door at 3-2.
  14. Approach the Hub exit at 11-2 to trigger the alarm.
  15. Return to the Med Room and pick up the supplies at 2-3.
  16. Go back to the Hub and use the exit at 11-2 to leave.

Arcadia Spaceport

  1. Enter the Transfer Area through the door at 7-1.
  2. In the Transfer Area, pick up the ammunition box at 10-3.
  3. Optional: listen to the Origin 1 recording at 11-7, facing right.
  4. Reach the Depot through the door at 2-5.
  5. In the Depot, pick up the medkit at 5-5.
  6. Pick up the ammunition box at 4-10.
  7. Return to the Transfer Area through the door at 8-14.
  8. Use the door at 6-1 to reach the Terminal.
  9. In the Terminal, optional: listen to the Origin 2 recording at 10-9, facing down.
  10. Take the train at 6-2.

Mining District

  1. From the Mining District Entrance, reach the Extraction Area through the door at 3-11.
  2. In the Extraction Area, stand in front of the panel at 2-5.
  3. Face down and press the interaction key to start the panel.
  4. Wait for the audio sequence to finish, then press the interaction key on the panel again to activate it.
  5. Return to the Entrance.
  6. From the Entrance, enter the Storage Area through the door at 13-2.
  7. Face up and hold the interaction key on Console 1 at 8-1 until the hold sequence finishes.
  8. For Console 2 at 11-13, face right and hold the interaction key in the same way. This is optional for progression, but unlocks both mining equipment items.
  9. Return to the Entrance and use the box at 1-6 to repair your shield.
  10. Enter the Extraction Area through the door at 3-11.
  11. Pick up the now-unlocked ammunition box at 1-9.
  12. Return to the Entrance through the door at 12-1.
  13. Re-enter the Storage Area.
  14. Enter the Crushing Area through the door at 5-15.
  15. Pick up the valve at 13-6.
  16. Return to the Storage Area to minimize your time in the Crushing Area and the risk of being intercepted by the Tank.
  17. Return to the Entrance.
  18. Take the train at 6-1 to the Terminal.

Underground Network

  1. From the Terminal, return to the Transfer Area (6-1). You will encounter Sigma again.
  2. From the Transfer Area, head to the Bridge (6-13).
  3. On the Bridge, descend through the manhole at 7-14 to enter the East Tunnel.
  4. Pick up the ammunition box at 12-5.
  5. Operate the lever at 4-5 with rapid, repeated presses until it is complete.
  6. From the East Tunnel, enter the West Tunnel through the door at 15-3.
  7. Use the box at 8-7 to repair your shield.
  8. Optional: listen to the Hacker recording at 12-6, facing down.
  9. Complete the second lever at 7-2 in the same way.
  10. Enter the Maintenance Room (6-8).
  11. To power the line, set the nine switches as follows:
    • Switch at 1-4: on.
    • Switch at 11-4: off.
    • Switch at 11-12: on.
    • Switch at 1-12: on.
    • Switch at 4-6: off.
    • Switch at 8-6: on.
    • Switch at 8-10: on.
    • Switch at 4-10: off.
    • Switch at 6-15: on.
  12. The circuit activates automatically when the combination is correct.
  13. Return to the West Tunnel and enter the Train Graveyard through the now-unlocked door at 12-1.
  14. Pick up the medkit at 2-12.
  15. Pick up the ammunition box at 14-1.
  16. Optional: listen to the Capacitor recording at 14-9, facing down.
  17. Pick up the navigation device at 2-9.
  18. The Capacitor will attack you. Try to reach the exit as quickly as possible, using your shield to block its attacks and do not shoot it.
  19. Return to the West Tunnel, then to the East Tunnel.
  20. Use the ladder at 8-6 to climb back up to the Bridge.
  21. From the Bridge, return to the Transfer Area and then the Terminal.
  22. Take the train; it now goes to the External Factory.

External Factory

  1. In the Entrance Area, pick up the Perforator ammunition at 19-6. Load the Perforator into your weapon, as it is the only ammunition that can take down enemies in the factory.
  2. Enter the factory through the door at 2-1.
  3. Pick up the ammunition box at 13-8.
  4. Hold the interaction key on the computer at 7-6 until the sequence finishes; this gives you access to the Circuit Section.
  5. Enter the Circuit Section through the door at 1-3.
  6. Turn off the machines using the switch at 10-1.
  7. Pick up the sensor at 3-5.
  8. Pick up the Radiator at 6-11.
  9. Return to the Components Center through the door at 14-8.
  10. Enter the Biology Lab via the elevator at 13-1. To deal with the last enemies in this room, slow them with the Overloader if you have run out of Perforator ammunition, or use the Radiator as a last resort.
  11. In the Biology Lab, optional: listen to the Interface recording at 14-2, facing right.
  12. Start the bed synchronization at 8-7 by holding the interaction key. Release it when the signal enters the correct window (about 7–8 seconds after starting).
  13. Escape the room to return to the Components Center.
  14. Leave the factory.
  15. Take the train at 3-22 back to Arcadia Spaceport.
  16. Reach the Bridge and return aboard the Neptune.

Logistics Complex

  1. In the Landing Hangar, pick up the ammunition box at 7-6.
  2. Optional: listen to the Squad recording at 18-6, facing right.
  3. Enter the Evacuation Zone through the door at 11-21.
  4. In the Evacuation Zone, use the box at 4-16 to repair your shield.
  5. Unlock the brake at 1-4.
  6. Return to the Landing Hangar and pick up the cable at 5-21.
  7. Re-enter the Evacuation Zone.
  8. Attach the cable to the capsule at 10-7.
  9. Attach the cable to the container by the door at 13-9.
  10. With both ends attached, complete the countdown at 10-16. Enter the warehouse through the newly unlocked door.
  11. In the Supplies Warehouse, a Tank will approach you. Take it out with a Detonator if you have one, or protect yourself while moving to the next room.
  12. Continue to the Rover Workshop through the door at 15-20.
  13. In the Rover Workshop, pick up the ammunition box at 18-7.
  14. Interact with the rover at 13-12.
  15. Pick up the battery at 1-13.
  16. Return to the Supplies Warehouse.
  17. Pick up the ammunition box at 8-10.
  18. Pick up the medkit at 12-15.
  19. Restore the electrolyte at 13-1 by holding the interaction key. Release it when the signal enters the correct window (about 7–8 seconds).
  20. Re-enter the Evacuation Zone.
  21. Charge the device at 1-13 with rapid, repeated presses of the interaction key.
  22. Return to the Rover Workshop.
  23. Interact with the rover at 13-12 again and leave for the Power Station.

Power Station

  1. In the Generator Room, pick up the Radiator at 15-1. Try to save it until the final game map, the Hydroponic Farm.
  2. Optional: listen to the Tripod recording at 21-5, facing right.
  3. Enter the Water Recycling Room through the door at 7-1.
  4. Use the box at 12-18 to repair your shield. Before moving to the next room, load the Magnetor if you have one; it will help if you encounter the Tripod. This enemy makes it difficult to identify the direction of its attack, and the Magnetor helps you avoid wasting shots. If you do not have Magnetor ammunition, either fire Plasma wildly or escape the room quickly.
  5. Continue to the Server Room through the door at 10-1.
  6. Pick up the ammunition box at 2-7.
  7. Optional: listen to the Squad recording at 9-5, facing right.
  8. Hold the interaction key on the computer at 15-5 until the hold sequence finishes.
  9. Enter the Waste Recycling Room through the door at 2-10.
  10. Pick up the ammunition box at 11-14.
  11. Interact with the exit door at 5-1 to hear the dialogue.
  12. Return to the Server Room through the door at 16-5.
  13. Pick up the bar at 1-3.
  14. From the Server Room, go to the Water Recycling Room.
  15. Pick up the pipe at 17-12.
  16. Return to the Generator Room.
  17. Open the cabinet at 2-5 and pick up the acid at 1-10.
  18. From the Generator Room, return to the Water Recycling Room.
  19. Create the acid mixture at 1-11.
  20. Return to the Server Room.
  21. Enter the Waste Recycling Room.
  22. Reach the exit door at 5-1 and break the chain with rapid, repeated presses, then go through the unlocked door.

Housing Unit

  1. In the Housing Unit's Main Room, move forward to trigger the opening event.
  2. Go to the Dormitory door at 5-1 to hear the dialogue.
  3. Enter the Bathroom through the door at 1-4.
  4. Pick up the ammunition box at 7-1.
  5. Pick up the clip at 5-5.
  6. Return to the Main Room and enter the Kitchen through the door at 1-9. There is a Tank in this room, so hurry and pick up the box at 8-1.
  7. Pick up the screwdriver at 8-8 and return to the Main Room.
  8. Enter the Dormitory through the door at 5-1. There is a Tripod in this room; defeat it with the Magnetor if you have it, or slow it with the Overloader.
  9. Optional: listen to the Gamma recording at 2-1, facing up.
  10. Optional: listen to the Tau recording at 2-5, facing up.
  11. Optional: listen to the Omega recording at 2-9, facing up.
  12. Interact with the tablet at 9-5 to hear the dialogue.
  13. Pick up the ammunition box at 7-1.
  14. Pick up the pencil at 9-11.
  15. Return to the Kitchen and pick up the tape at 4-3.
  16. Pick up the brush in the Bathroom at 1-2.
  17. Return to the Dormitory tablet at 9-5 and hold the interaction key to obtain the fingerprint.
  18. Return to the Main Room and use the Underground Laboratory door at 13-4.

Underground Laboratory

  1. Go down the stairs to hear the dialogue.
  2. Pick up the Magnetor ammunition at 4-11.
  3. Enter the Research Center through the door at 1-15.
  4. Set the three switches to the following values:
    • Power at 3-9: 2 milliwatts.
    • Wave at 3-13: 450 nanometers.
    • Temperature at 3-11: 22°.
  5. Start the download on the computer at 8-9. Hold the interaction key until it is complete.
  6. Optional: listen to the Tau recording at 11-13, facing right.
  7. Pick up the ammunition box at 10-9.
  8. Return to the Control Room.
  9. Enter the Climate Center through the door at 14-15.
  10. Pick up the ammunition box at 9-5.
  11. Optional: listen to the Omega recording at 5-12, facing down.
  12. At the computer at 7-9, adjust the satellite aim: increase the azimuth from 220 to 280 (six increments) and the elevation from 10 to 40 (three increments).
  13. Return to the Main Room.
  14. Enter the Hydroponic Farm through the door at 8-20. There is a Tank in this room: use a Radiator or prepare to use a Detonator or Overloader.
  15. Use the box at 1-6 to repair your shield.
  16. Optional: listen to the Gamma recording at 6-8, facing left.
  17. Start the download on the computer at 14-8. Hold the interaction key until it is complete.
  18. Return to the Control Room door at 8-2 to trigger the alarm.
  19. During the alarm, close the tank at 9-15 with rapid, repeated presses of the interaction key.
  20. Start the ventilation by holding the interaction key on the air device at 1-11.
  21. Complete the maneuver quickly, as the toxic air reduces your health.
  22. Return to the Main Room to witness the final scene. You can now choose:
    • Ending 1: hold the interaction key on the terminal at 8-16 to erase all data.
    • Ending 2: leave the Underground Laboratory.
